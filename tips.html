Tips & Tricks

Get your hands dirty—in the best way. From glaze guides to clay hacks, explore bite-sized lessons and studio wisdom for every stage of your ceramics journey.

1. Designing Functional Vases

When shaping ceramic vases, always consider weight balance and wall thickness to ensure stability while holding water and flowers.

2. Perfecting Matte White Glaze

Achieving a smooth white stoneware finish requires uniform glaze application and slow cooling in the kiln to avoid bubbling.

3. Handcrafted Clay Jars & Pitchers

For consistent pottery sets, measure your clay weight before pulling on the wheel to keep heights and proportions uniform.
